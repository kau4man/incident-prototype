              30-second brief

              What to try

              1. Click any underlined phrase in the story to see the evidence behind it.
              2. In the “Needs you” card, press and hold “Hold to approve”. Or pick another option, decline, or just let the timer run out.
              3. After you decide, wait a few seconds: the flow logs arrive and the “unknown” in the story turns into a fact.
              4. Hover over timeline events and click any of them. Everything to the right of the “now” line is a forecast.
              5. Select “No other endpoint ran the package” and challenge it with “Dispute this”.
              6. Open commands from the field at the bottom or with ⌘K: the investigation trace, a 30-second brief, a new check.
              7. Undo any action in “Done without you” and see what the agent asks.
              8. The time buttons at the top switch between moments of the incident. “Morning” shows the wrap-up view.
